## 🚀 Where It All Started {: #origin }

I still remember walking into a robotics expo as a child and being completely blown away. The blinking LEDs, the moving joints, the feeling that I was witnessing the **future** — that moment stuck with me. Ever since, I’ve been hooked on sci-fi, cyberpunk, and everything that feels like it belongs in a tomorrow that’s already here.

Before I even finished high school, I was already building rudimentary programs, full of bugs and excitement. I remember thinking, _“I could do this for the rest of my life.”_ So I did.

---

## 🛠 What I Do (and Love Doing) {: #what-i-do }

I build complex backend systems. It’s where I feel most at home — architecting infrastructure, optimizing processes, and spinning up APIs and services that hum with efficiency. I’ve spent over a decade crafting digital platforms for everything from social good to business logistics.

My main stack today centers around **Python** (which I love for its clarity and flexibility), and I deploy most of my solutions on **AWS**, where spinning up EC2 instances and wiring up scalable architecture feels like second nature.

Over the years, I’ve also become really fast — and really good — at shipping web applications and APIs from scratch. Startup life taught me that adaptability is a skill, and learning new tech is just part of the fun.

---

## 🌍 Code With Purpose {: #purpose }

Problem-solving has always been my engine. But more recently, it’s become about more than just logic — it’s about **impact**. I’ve worked with organizations like the **World Bank Group** on digital tools that help communities in Africa connect with governments, track climate vulnerabilities, and promote peace. In Venezuela, I’ve supported social research by building data collection apps used in the field.

For me, the real reward is seeing tech solve human problems.
